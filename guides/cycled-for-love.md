# He Cycled to Europe for Love (about 45s, 5 scenes)

Real story: in 1975 in New Delhi, Charlotte von Schedvin from Sweden asked street artist P.K. Mahanandia to draw her portrait. They fell in love. He couldn't afford a ticket to Sweden, so he sold everything, bought a bicycle and rode thousands of miles over about four months, through eight countries, to be with her. They're still together about 50 years later.
The real photos (young and old) are in the reference video and get used for the ending.

Characters: young artist (Indian man, 20s, curly black hair, mustache, 1970s clothes) and young Charlotte (Swedish woman, 20s, long straight blonde hair, 1970s clothes). Both are adults, so Flow should allow them.
