# Heartwarming Holly: Facebook page setup and growth

Built from E'Calm's "How I Make $1,024,937 In 90 Days Posting Facebook Reels" (read from a third-party transcript; his video wouldn't load here) and the earlier Holly planning session. **His income numbers are his own claims and nobody has checked them.**

## 1. Set up the page (one time)

1. Use your normal Facebook profile. Go to **Menu → See more → Pages → Create**.
2. **Name:** search terms work better than a personal name. Keep **Heartwarming Holly**, since it already starts with the main search term ("heartwarming"). For a second page later, build the name from keywords as well, for example "Kindness Stories Daily" or "True Stories in 3D".
3. **Category:** Digital creator. In settings, set the page type to **Creator**.
4. **Bio:** don't leave it empty. This one was chosen in the earlier session (90 characters):
   > Heartwarming true stories retold in 3D animation, then revealed with the real viral video.
5. **Profile and cover pictures:** use real pictures you choose, not generic AI images. A Holly-style 3D character with the HOLLY logo works well because it matches the videos.
6. **Contact info:** fill it in. Skip the WhatsApp step.
7. **Don't invite all your friends.** If people who don't care about the topic follow, Facebook shows your videos to the wrong audience. Let the page grow on its own.
8. **Don't boost posts with ads.** Organic growth only.

## 2. Keywords

Use these in the page name, the bio, each video's title and its caption:

- Main: **heartwarming stories**, **true story**, **kindness**, **wholesome**, **feel good**
- Topic, depending on the story: **rescue**, **reunion**, **strangers**, **teacher**, **dad**, **mom**, **dog**, **surprise**
- Check a keyword by typing it into Facebook search. Lots of pages and videos means people search for it. Pick ones where you can stand out.
- E'Calm used vidIQ's **Research** tab to compare how much a term is searched against how many others use it. That's optional.

## 3. Each post

- **Upload as a Reel**, not a regular video post. Reels get shown to people who don't follow you yet.
- **Rename the file** to the keyword title before uploading. E'Calm says not to leave the default name.
- **Title:** the hook, plus an emoji at the end like Wendy's, under about 60 characters. Example: *A Stranger Handed Him Flowers… Then He Read the Note 💐*
- **Caption:** 1 line of feeling, plus credit, plus 3–5 hashtags:
  > He had no idea a stranger could see what he was carrying. ❤️ Credit to the original creator for the real video.
  > #heartwarming #kindness #wholesome #truestory #feelgood
- **Audience:** Public.
- **AI label:** turn on Meta's AI label. The videos are clearly 3D, so it costs you nothing, and pages that hide AI use risk losing monetization.

## 4. How often to post

- **At least 3 a day** once you have the stories ready. E'Calm posts 3–15 a day across his pages.
- To start, 1–2 a day while the process gets faster is fine. Keep a steady pace; don't post 10 one day and nothing for a week.
- Story order: alternate oldest and newest. Skip anything Wendy posted in the last 2 weeks.

## 5. What Facebook rewards

- E'Calm says Facebook tests each Reel with a small group, then shows it to more people if the **likes, comments, shares and saves** are good. That's why the hook matters so much.
- Ending on a question helps comments ("Would you have done the same?").
- Pages without a clear topic get stuck around 100–200 views per video. Keep every video on-topic: true kindness stories in 3D.

## 6. Getting paid

- Facebook monetization is **invite-only**. Check **Professional dashboard → Monetization** for an invite.
- E'Calm's rough target is **5,000 followers and 60,000 minutes viewed** in 60 days. That's his estimate, not Facebook's official rule, and Facebook changes its rules often. Other sources have said 10,000 followers and 600,000 minutes.
- Reels pay roughly **$0.02–$0.20 per 1,000 views**. That comes from one guide and varies a lot, so it takes volume.

## 7. What gets a page restricted (important for remaking Wendy's stories)

Facebook restricts pages that post **"unoriginal" or reposted content**. To protect Holly:

- **Work from the original real video, not Wendy's short.** Write your own hook, captions and shots.
- **Use a different hook than hers** for the same story.
- **Keep your own look:** Holly characters, neon outfits, the HOLLY watermark, Jesse's voice, and your side-by-side real-footage ending.
- **Credit the original creator** of the real clip in every caption.
- Don't post a story within a couple of weeks of Wendy posting it.
