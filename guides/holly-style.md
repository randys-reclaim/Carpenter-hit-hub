# Heartwarming Holly house style

Updated after Randy's feedback: the neon was too bright and the paneled clothes looked like plastic. The look is now warm, natural color with real fabric, and "Heartwarming Holly" shows up around the scenes.

## Character image style (start of every character prompt)
Stylized 3D animated character in a Pixar/Blender feature-film look, slightly exaggerated features but grounded and believable. Matte skin with natural texture. Hair sculpted with soft strands, not a solid helmet. Clothing made of real fabric: soft cotton, worn denim, knit polos, canvas shorts, with natural folds, seams and stitching. Warm, natural color palette with gentle saturation; colors can be rich but never neon or glowing. No glossy or plastic-looking clothes.

## Character image ending (end of every character prompt)
Neutral expression, standing, full body, facing camera, plain warm-grey studio background, soft even lighting, vertical 9:16.

## Clip style (start of every video prompt)
Stylized 3D animated video in a Pixar/Blender feature-film look, matte skin with natural texture, soft sculpted hair, real fabric clothing (cotton, denim, knit) with natural folds, warm natural color palette with gentle saturation (no neon, nothing glowing or plastic), soft cinematic lighting, expressive faces, vertical 9:16, shallow depth of field, slight handheld shake, no subtitles, no captions, no dialogue, ambient sound only. The only readable text in the scene is "HEARTWARMING HOLLY" or "HOLLY" where described.

## Branding: "Heartwarming Holly" around the scenes
Put the brand in 2–3 places per short, always on something that belongs in the scene:
- **Clothing:** a ball cap or t-shirt with a small "HEARTWARMING HOLLY" print, an apron or name tag with "HOLLY"
- **Signs:** a storefront, diner or food-truck sign reading "HEARTWARMING HOLLY", or "HOLLY'S DINER" / "HOLLY'S PIZZA"
- **Props:** a coffee mug, a bumper sticker, a cardboard box, a grocery bag, a newspaper headline
- **Food:** "HOLLY" in frosting, sprinkles or olives
- **Sports and events:** a jersey, banner, scoreboard or ticket: "HOLLY ARENA", "HEARTWARMING HOLLY"

Rules:
- On small things (a shirt print, mug, cap or name tag in a full-body shot), use just **"HOLLY"**. Tested: "HOLLY" comes out clean, but small "HEARTWARMING" gets garbled.
- Use the full **"HEARTWARMING HOLLY"** only where it's big in the frame: a storefront sign, a banner, a truck side, or a close-up of the shirt or mug.
- Video models still garble long words sometimes. For a clip, use the full name only when it's large and in its own close-up; otherwise use "HOLLY". Make the branded thing the start frame, so the text comes from the image.
- If it comes out misspelled, regenerate that shot or cut around it.
- The full name also stays in the watermark, which the editing tool adds.
