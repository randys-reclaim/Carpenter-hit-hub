# The Truckers Saved Him (about 50s, 6 clips)

Reference: Wholesome Wendy, "The Truckers Saved Him" (3.8M views). Real event: truckers boxed in a car carrying a kidnapped 2-year-old near a state border until a trooper arrived, and the boy was found unharmed.

Kid note: the toddler is never a main character. He's only shown from behind in a car seat, and in the trooper's arms from behind. This keeps Flow from blocking the clips.

## Characters
TRUCKER: Stylized 3D animated character, super layered, hyperrealistic but almost cartoonish. Matte skin, arms slightly long, legs thin. Hair and eyebrows sculpted as solid pieces. Clothing made of cartoonish solid panels in bright neon colors, with glossy layered blacks for depth. A man in his thirties with short brown hair and light stubble, a kind steady face, a neon red t-shirt with a small white HOLLY logo on the chest, glossy black jeans and work boots. Standing, full body, facing the camera, neutral expression, plain grey studio background, soft even lighting, vertical.

KIDNAPPER: same style opening. A man in his forties with a shaved head, a tense nervous face, a glossy black hoodie and dark jeans. Standing, full body, facing the camera, neutral expression, plain grey studio background, soft even lighting, vertical.

SECOND TRUCKER: same style opening. A heavyset man in his forties with a thick brown beard and a trucker cap, a neon grey t-shirt, glossy black jeans. Standing, full body, facing the camera, neutral expression, plain grey studio background, soft even lighting, vertical.

TROOPER: same style opening. A state trooper in his thirties in a crisp neon-trimmed navy uniform with a wide-brim trooper hat and glossy black boots. Standing, full body, facing the camera, neutral expression, plain grey studio background, soft even lighting, vertical.

## VO (Jesse)
He was just hauling a load down the interstate… when a car pulled up beside him. It looked exactly like the one police had just warned about. A kidnapped two-year-old. He checked the plate. It matched. The car was doing over eighty… racing for the state line. One trooper, miles behind. So he grabbed his radio… and called every trucker ahead of him. One by one, the rigs closed in. Front. Sides. Back. Slower… and slower… until the car had nowhere left to go. The trooper caught up. The man gave up. And the little boy was safe. The truckers never stopped. They just kept driving. When a reporter told him he'd saved that boy's life, he only said one thing. "I'm just glad he got to go home."
