# How Wendy shoots and edits (from "The Truckers Saved Him")

Taken from her trucker/kidnapper short: 53 seconds, vertical, about 3.6M views. Use this for the camera and editing in every Holly short. **Holly keeps its own look** (neon paneled clothing, matte skin, HOLLY branding). Only the camera and edit style come from Wendy.

## The rhythm
- **A new shot every 1–2 seconds.** In 53 seconds, almost every second shows a different angle. Nothing sits still.
- Most cuts happen **during camera movement**: a whip-pan blur, a push into a face, the camera flying past something. Cuts barely show because the motion carries over.
- **Length:** 45–55 seconds for a story with action (chase, rescue). 20–30 seconds for a simple kindness moment.

## Shot types she rotates through
| Shot | What it looks like | When she uses it |
|---|---|---|
| **Drone wide** | High and far back, the road or place below | Opening, a new location, big events |
| **Low front** | Camera low in front of a vehicle or person moving toward the lens | Introducing the hero (the truck coming at you) |
| **Chase / tracking** | Behind a car, following it down the road | Any pursuit or "following" beat |
| **Top-down** | Straight down from above, objects moving below | The trap or moment where it comes together (trucks boxing the car in) |
| **Face push-in** | Fast zoom into an eye-level close-up | Reactions: shock, fear, realizing something |
| **Whip-pan** | Camera snaps sideways with heavy motion blur | Transitions between beats |
| **In-cab / over the shoulder** | Behind the driver, through the windshield, hand on the radio or phone | Decisions ("he radioed", "called 911") |
| **Detail insert** | License plate, phone screen, hand, note | Proof the story is real |
| **Big text pop-up** | A huge 3D word or number in the shot ("80 MPH") | Key facts: speed, money, age |
| **Interview** | Hero holding a microphone, reporter half in frame | Last 5–8 seconds, the hero's own words |
| **Real-footage reveal** | Real news photo or clip, full screen or side by side | Very last 1–2 seconds |

## Captions
- **1–2 words at a time**, big and bold, in the center of the screen.
- **Each word in a different color:** yellow, magenta, cyan, green, red, white. Not only keywords; the color changes with almost every word.
- Black outline, no box.
- Watermark: small, at the bottom center, the whole time.

## Branding
- The hero wears a **red t-shirt with a big "W" logo**, and the logo is on the truck too. That's the same idea as HOLLY: one hero shirt with the logo, plus the logo on 1–2 objects.

## The story beats (53 seconds)
1. **0–8s, hero and setup:** a trucker on the interstate hears a kidnapping alert on the radio.
2. **8–18s, the danger:** the matching car, the license plate, "80 MPH," the scared kid in the back.
3. **18–27s, the plan:** he radios other truckers, they surround the car and slow it down, he calls 911.
4. **27–38s, the takedown:** trucks box the car in from above, police arrive, the kid is safe.
5. **38–50s, aftermath:** news on TV, the mom finds him, the hero's interview ("I'm just glad…").
6. **50–53s, the real footage.**

## Camera lines to put in clip prompts
Add 3–4 of these to each 8-second clip, one per 2-second beat:
- `[0-2s] fast drone shot sweeping down toward ___`
- `[0-2s] low camera in front of ___ as it drives straight at the lens`
- `[2-4s] tracking shot behind ___, following it down the road`
- `[2-4s] fast push-in to an extreme close-up of ___'s face, shocked`
- `[4-6s] whip-pan with heavy motion blur to ___`
- `[4-6s] top-down view looking straight down as ___`
- `[6-8s] over-the-shoulder inside the cab, his hand grabbing the radio`
- `[6-8s] close-up insert of ___ (license plate / phone screen / note)`

Text graphics like "80 MPH" and the real-footage ending are added in editing, not in the prompt, so they're spelled correctly every time.
