# Wrong Number Thanksgiving (about 35s, 4 scenes)

Verified: in November 2016 in Arizona, Wanda Dench meant to text her grandson a Thanksgiving invite but texted a stranger, Jamal Hinton (then 17). He asked if he could still get a plate. She said, "Of course you can. That's what grandmas do." He came, and they've spent Thanksgiving together almost every year since. Netflix announced a movie about them.
Jamal is drawn as a young adult (about 20) so Flow allows it.
