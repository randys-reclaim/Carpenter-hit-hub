# The Counterfeit Bag, Part 1: "The English Kid"

First-person true story, told by Randy. About 55–60 seconds, vertical.
Series plan: Part 1 is high school and how it started; later parts build up to
the Mercedes and the field with the shovel (Randy's "YouTube Short Voice
Script" is the ending).

## Voice script

Have you ever been somewhere…
where the next thing you say decides where you sleep for the next five to ten years?
I have.
But to understand how I ended up there…
you gotta go back to high school.

I was the new kid.
Moved from a little country town in East Texas…
to Garland. Right outside Dallas.
Five 5A high schools. Big.

I was the cool kid.
I had friends that were popular…
and friends that weren't so much.
So I did what cool kids do.
I introduced them to each other.

We all played football.
As a team? We were bad.
As individuals… a few of us were alright.

Then two of my buddies, Chris and Tim…
picked up a new friend.
A kid from England.
We called him Mate.

I never really liked Mate.
You know those movies…
the clean-cut English schoolboy.
Short hair. "Yes sir. No ma'am."
And behind your back… he's the one starting the fire.
That was Mate.
Angus Young from AC/DC…
with Eddie Haskell manners.

Mate got in with Chris and Tim.
Tim got distant.
I was doing my own thing… so I didn't pay much attention.
Looking back?
I'm glad I didn't.

Because on the cool…
they were doing some unbelievably bad stuff.
Money.
Helicopters.
New cars.
Drugs.
Loud school nights.

And somehow…
I'm the one who ended up in a field…
with a shovel.

Part two.

## Scenes (3D Holly style, about 10 clips)

1. **Hook:** close-up of a nervous young man in a dark field at night, two men in suits standing behind him, a shovel at his feet.
2. **Country to city:** a pickup truck leaving a small East Texas farm town, then the Dallas skyline at sunset.
3. **Big school:** a huge Texas high school, students flooding the halls; Randy as the new kid, confident grin.
4. **Introducing friends:** Randy at a lunch table pulling a popular group and a nerdy group together, everyone laughing.
5. **Football:** a muddy high school football game, the team fumbling; one player making a great solo tackle.
6. **Mate arrives:** a clean-cut English schoolboy with short hair and a polite smile shaking hands with Chris and Tim.
7. **The two faces of Mate:** Mate saying "yes ma'am" to a teacher, then turning around with a sly grin, holding a lighter.
8. **Tim drifting away:** Tim walking off with Mate and Chris; Randy watching from his locker, shrugging.
9. **Montage, one quick shot each:** a stack of cash, a helicopter over a football field, a shiny new sports car in the school lot, a loud house party on a school night.
10. **Cliffhanger:** back to the dark field; the shovel hits the dirt. Black screen: "PART 2".

## Notes

- **Names:** Chris, Tim and Mate are real people. Because the story involves drugs and counterfeit money, consider changing their names or adding "names changed" on screen.
- **Movie references:** Angus Young and Eddie Haskell are mentioned in the voiceover only. Don't put real clips or pictures of them in the video (copyright); the 3D Mate character shows the idea.
