# The Counterfeit Bag, Part 2: "The Red Honda"

First-person true story, told by Randy. About 45–55 seconds, vertical.

## Voice script

Last time…
I told you I ended up in a field with a shovel.
This is the night I should've seen it coming.

My class was different.
As freshmen… we threw our own parties.
And we didn't let the older guys in.
Just their girlfriends.
If they had a problem with that…
we handled it.
Freshmen aren't supposed to win those fights.
We did.
We went to their parties.
If it got rowdy…
we left with their kegs.

So yeah.
I had a tough class.
And Tim and Chris?
Two of the toughest.

So one night I'm walking into a party.
Older crowd.
Cool… but different.
And this little red Honda comes flying up.
Brand new.
Doors open…
and it's Tim and Chris.
Gracing us with their presence.

No big deal, right?
Except…
I knew their families.
And they didn't have new-car money.
They didn't have any car money.

So now I'm interested.
But they're acting weird.
Too cool for school.
So I blew them off.

That…
was my first mistake.

Part three.

## Scenes (3D Holly style, about 9 clips)

1. **Recap hook:** the dark field from Part 1, a shovel stuck in the dirt, a flashlight sweeping across it.
2. **Freshman party:** a packed house party of young teens; older guys stopped at the front door, their girlfriends walking right past them.
3. **Standoff:** freshmen squaring up to bigger senior guys on a front lawn at night (tense stare-down, no blood).
4. **The kegs:** a group of freshmen running down a driveway laughing, carrying two beer kegs over their shoulders.
5. **Tough class:** slow walk of the freshman crew down a school hallway; Tim and Chris in front, arms crossed.
6. **Older crowd party:** Randy walking up to a big suburban house party at night, music and lights.
7. **The red Honda:** a shiny brand-new little red Honda flies into the driveway and skids to a stop; the doors open and Tim and Chris step out in sunglasses at night.
8. **Randy's face:** close-up of Randy squinting at the car, then at them (thinking about the money).
9. **Cliffhanger:** Randy turns and walks into the party; behind him the red Honda's headlights stay on, pointed at him. Black screen: "PART 3".

## Notes

- The fights stay off screen as stare-downs; no hitting, which keeps it safe for monetization.
- "First mistake" sets up Part 3. Tell Randy's next beat: when did he find out where the money came from?
