# The Counterfeit Bag, Part 3: "The Bank Bag"

First-person true story, told by Randy. About 55–60 seconds, vertical.

## Voice script

Summer. End of senior year.
I was working…
going to parties…
and dabbling in some stuff I wish I'd never met.

Then I hear about some money.
Some guys had a lot of it.
They might be in trouble…
and they needed it gone.

I didn't know the whole story.
I just knew…
we had a bag full of money.

A guy named Scott was holding it.
Kind of a needy guy Chris let into the group.
A crazy dude from a Dallas school had some first…
but he got in trouble.
Somebody was hot on that money's trail.
So he passed it to Scott.

Then Scott hands me a bank bag.
Full.
I'm looking at it like…
this is it.
I'm gonna help my family out.
I thought long and hard about it…
and I decided not to tell my dad.

That night, Scott and I go out.
A club. Our buddy's tending bar.
Scott hands him one bill…
and gets real change back.
That's laundering.
Funny money in… real money out.

Oh.
Did I not tell y'all?
It was counterfeit.
Not novelty-store money.
This was as real as it gets.
So real…
they didn't just want the money.
They wanted whoever made it.

And I had a bag of it…
under my bed.

Part four.

## Scenes (3D Holly style, about 10 clips)

1. **Summer:** Randy in a work uniform, sweating in the Texas sun, then at a backyard party at night.
2. **Whispers:** two guys whispering to Randy at a party, glancing around nervously.
3. **The bag travels:** a wild-eyed guy in a Dallas school jacket tossing a bag to Scott in a parking lot, police lights flashing far behind him.
4. **Scott:** an eager, nervous young guy holding the bag tight against his chest.
5. **The bank bag:** Randy unzipping a grey bank bag stuffed with crisp bills, his face lit up green.
6. **Dad:** Randy standing outside his dad's door, hand raised to knock… then lowering it and walking away.
7. **The club:** a dim neon bar; the bartender friend slides change across the counter to Scott; Randy watching.
8. **Funny money in, real money out:** close-up of one crisp bill going across the bar and a stack of worn real bills coming back.
9. **The reveal:** a magnifying glass over a bill; it looks perfect. Text pops: "COUNTERFEIT".
10. **Cliffhanger:** the bank bag under Randy's bed in the dark; the camera slowly pushes in. Black screen: "PART 4".
