# The Counterfeit Bag, Part 4: "The Raid"

First-person true story, told by Randy. About 55–60 seconds, vertical.
Ends where Randy's original short script ("Have you ever been in a position…")
begins; that script is Part 5, the finale.

## Voice script

Scott and I partied till the bar closed.
I was proud of us.
We went home.
I'd barely fallen asleep…

BOOM.
Sounded like a shotgun.

I was upstairs, in my little brother's room.
The front door downstairs?
Kicked in.
I'm at the balcony… about to jump…
and I hear,
"Where's he at? Upstairs!"
Flashlights coming up the stairs.

They're tearing my brother's room apart.
I'm yelling, "Stop! It's not my room!"
My sister's crying next door.
All they keep saying is,
"Where's it at?"

I'm standing there in my boxers.
"Get dressed. You're coming with us."
I said, "No, I'm not."
They said, "Yes. You are."
I yell for my dad.
He says, "Shut up and do what they say."
So… I guess I am.

Downstairs, the door's hanging off the hinges.
My mom, my dad, my little sister…
out in the yard in their pajamas.
The whole cul-de-sac is watching.

I told him, "Dad, whatever this is… I didn't do it."
He said, "I know, son. That's what I told them."
He didn't hesitate.
Not for one second.

They walk me to a little burnt-orange Mercedes.
And across the cul-de-sac…
there's a car.
Scott's sitting in it.
He gave them my name.

We pull off. Wrong way down a one-way street.
I say something.
And one of them goes,
"We can do whatever we want.
We're Secret Service."

I thought they were just cops.
That was bad enough.

Part five.

## Scenes (3D Holly style, about 11 clips)

1. **Last call:** Randy and Scott stumbling out of a neon bar at closing time, laughing.
2. **Asleep:** Randy asleep in a kid's bedroom (toys, small bed), moonlight through the window.
3. **BOOM:** the front door blasting off its hinges, splinters flying; white flash.
4. **Balcony:** Randy at the upstairs balcony rail in boxers, one leg over, frozen.
5. **Flashlights:** beams sweeping up a dark staircase; silhouettes of men in suits.
6. **The search:** agents flipping the little brother's mattress and dumping drawers; Randy yelling.
7. **Sister:** a teenage girl crying in a doorway down the hall.
8. **The yard:** Randy's mom, dad and little sister in pajamas on the front lawn at night; neighbors watching from their porches.
9. **Dad:** close-up of Randy's dad, arms crossed, calm and firm, nodding at his son.
10. **Scott:** a small burnt-orange Mercedes in the driveway; across the cul-de-sac, Scott slumped low in a parked car, looking away.
11. **Cliffhanger:** the Mercedes speeding the wrong way down a one-way street past a "ONE WAY" sign; an agent in sunglasses turns to camera. Text: "SECRET SERVICE". Black screen: "PART 5".
