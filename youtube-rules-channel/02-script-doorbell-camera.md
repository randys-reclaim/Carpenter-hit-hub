## Why this topic should perform

- **Keyword:** "doorbell camera" and "Ring doorbell footage" are huge, evergreen search and suggested-video categories. Real doorbell-cam compilations already pull tens of millions of views, so YouTube knows exactly who to show this to.
- **Ownership:** Doorbell cameras are in tens of millions of homes. The "check your own app" moment makes viewers *physically* open their phone, which drives comments ("I just checked… only one 😰").
- **Thumbnail:** A doorbell-cam fisheye frame is instantly recognizable at phone size.
- **"That's why" payoffs (comment fuel):** full cloud storage, the phone dying while charging, déjà vu, a moved car seat, a high step count, "you seemed different."
- **Series potential:** "The Residents' Office" can return in every video (smart fridge, Wi-Fi, robot vacuum), and so can the "Shared Users" concept.

## Production notes for this episode

- **VO pacing:** Read flat and calm, like a pharmacy safety leaflet. Never act "spooky." Leave real silence on `//` pauses. Silence is what makes it creepy.
- **SFX:** doorbell chime (pitched down about 5% for the 6 AM ring), a phone notification "ding" (use the same one every time; it becomes a trigger), keypad beeps, kettle whistle, room tone. Add a low drone bed at -28 dB.
- **Music:** None, or a single sustained pad. The format works *because* it's quiet.
- **Length check:** If the VO comes in under 7:45, add 2–3 more "that's why" lines to Rule 4 or Rule 6 rather than slowing the read.
