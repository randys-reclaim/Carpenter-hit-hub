## Why this topic should perform

- **The search is huge and comes back every winter.** The tire pressure light is one of the most-searched dashboard warnings. Searches spike every cold snap, so this video can get a fresh push from search every November through February.
- **Nearly every driver has lived it.** Cars sold in the U.S. since 2008 have a tire-pressure warning system. Almost everyone watching has seen the light on a cold morning.
- **It has more "that's why" payoffs than the doorbell script:** the light on cold mornings and off by lunch, tires losing air with no hole, water from the air hose, the car pulling right, the cupholder slosh, and knowing every free-air station by heart. These are what make people comment.
- **The real facts are verifiable:** about 1 PSI per 10°F, about 1 PSI lost per month, compressors making water, nitrogen fills, and California and Connecticut free-air laws. When viewers check, it lands as "wait, that's true", which builds trust in the rest.
- **Comedy plus creep.** Tires that are "needy" are funny and shareable. That fits the dog, cat and phone videos that perform best on the channel you're modeling.
