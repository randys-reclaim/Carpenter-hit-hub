# YouTube "Rules" Channel Kit

Research and production kit for a minimalist "If Your ___ Ever ___… DO NOT ___" rules-horror channel. It is modeled on the format of Bradikinin (@BradikininVideos), with an original identity.

1. [`01-channel-breakdown.md`](01-channel-breakdown.md): channel stats, both sample videos decoded, the beat-sheet template, why it goes viral, and 20 original title ideas
2. [`02-script-doorbell-camera.md`](02-script-doorbell-camera.md): a full 8-minute script with on-screen visuals, title, description, tags and chapters
3. [`03-visual-style-guide.md`](03-visual-style-guide.md): palette, fonts, frame layout, thumbnail formula and motion recipe
4. [`04-script-tire-pressure-light.md`](04-script-tire-pressure-light.md): Notice 004, "If Your Tire Pressure Light Ever Comes On… DON'T PULL OVER", a full 8-minute script built around your draft ending
5. [`storyboard/`](storyboard/): rendered thumbnail and key-frame mockups (`storyboard.html` is the source)
